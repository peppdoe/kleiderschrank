## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Clearly authored for this product. The care-label world carries through: seasonal name band with stitch, laundry symbols as seasons, kraft swing tags, sewn-on look labels, the toast's dashed "Rückgängig". Nothing here could be lifted unchanged into another app. Risk: the band is so beautiful that its season row reads as embroidery, not as the "Heute" control.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect --json src index.html` → 0 findings (exit 0) after DESIGN.md moved to the rem ramp; before that refresh it flagged 31 off-ramp font sizes and one 18px radius (both resolved). No false positives.

**Visual overlays:** detect.js injected in the [Human] tab on wardrobe, looks, piece detail and settings: "No anti-patterns found" on all four.

## Overall Impression
The app now forgives: delete is undoable, the first start asks her name, several pieces go in one after another, and the text grows with her iPhone. The biggest remaining gap is finding one specific piece among many: names exist, but she cannot type one.

## What's Working
- Undo in the toast instead of a second confirmation chain; the action keeps the quiet-link language and never turns red.
- The first run now has a warm two-step: one red action, then a quiet "Wie heißt du?" behind a dashed seam; the band answers immediately.
- Dynamic Type without breaking the world: rem ramp, capped fixed rows, inputs ≥16px; no horizontal overflow at 28px base.

## Priority Issues
- **[P2] Undo disappears on a fixed 6s timer** — Why: VoiceOver and slow hands may not reach "Rückgängig" in time; the toast vanishes even while the button has focus. Fix: pause the timer while the toast has focus or a pointer, and keep it until the next navigation for screen-reader users. Command: /impeccable harden
- **[P2] No search for names or notes** — Why: with 100+ pieces Lena knows "der grüne Blazer" but must filter by category and colour and scan photos. Fix: a search field in the filter sheet (or above the grid) over name, note and category label. Command: /impeccable shape
- **[P2] The season row on the band is not self-evident as a control** — Why: the "Heute" mechanism is the morning shortcut, but the symbols look like printed care instructions. Fix: a one-time hint line ("Tipp eine Saison an, dann zeigt Heute ihre Looks") or a subtle pressed affordance on first launch with pieces. Command: /impeccable onboard
- **[P3] "Heute" row cuts the second look name mid-word** — Why: "Herbstspaziergar" reads as a bug before it reads as "scroll sideways". Fix: fade the right edge or end the peeking item's name with an ellipsis. Command: /impeccable polish
- **[P3] Help only as one-liners** — Why: the separate Safari/home-screen storage and the two-step backup are the riskiest moments. Fix: a short "So sicherst du deinen Schrank" sheet in the settings. Command: /impeccable onboard

## Persona Red Flags
**Casey (one hand, morning):** primary actions in thumb reach everywhere; season row sits at the very top of the band, the only control out of thumb reach.
**Sam (VoiceOver):** status region announces toasts; "Rückgängig" is time-limited (6s) with no extension.
**Jordan / Lena on first start:** clear single action, name ask works; nothing explains that the band symbols change "Heute".
