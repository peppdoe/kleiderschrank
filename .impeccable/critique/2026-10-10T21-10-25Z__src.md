## Design Specificity Verdict
Mostly category-interchangeable (~2/5). Right bones (photo grid home, photo-led detail, outfit photo strips), but stock iOS-settings chrome (system greys, pill chips, emoji tab icons, uppercase micro-labels). Nothing addresses the "what do I wear today" moment; outfits are never shown as a composed look; the rose accent is spent on admin banners.
Detector: CLI 0 findings. Browser: line-length on install banner (~90 chars/line; agrees with A), nested-cards on #/settings (not reproducible, likely false positive). Measurements: all tap targets ≥44px; all text contrast AA (light muted on bg 4.54:1 borderline); no custom focus styles — A found FilePicker label/hidden-input pattern leaves FAB, "Foto wählen", "Backup einspielen" with no visible focus.

## What's Working
1. Warm, specific German copy; actionable error messages.
2. Accessibility baseline: 44px targets, aria-pressed, labelled tiles, role=alert/status, AA contrast.
3. Domain-aware robustness: delete warns about outfits, empty outfits handled, backup reminder.
